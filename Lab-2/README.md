# Lab 2: Epics, User Stories and Sprints

Agile planning for **Problem 6**, the Alumni Mentorship & Mock Interview Platform.

## What I did

- **Six epics with eleven user stories**, each written as *As a… I want to… So that…* and traced to the Lab 1 requirements:
  1. Account Access & Profile Management (secure login, alumni profile setup)
  2. Intelligent Mentor Matching (domain and preference entry, recommendation list)
  3. Mentor Availability Calendar
  4. Session Booking & Scheduling (view open slots, book and confirm, double-booking prevention)
  5. Post-Interview Scorecard (submit a scorecard, scorecard privacy)
  6. Student Mentorship Dashboard
- **Story points** for every story (50 SP in total).
- **Two sprints** with goals, sprint boards and burndown/report screenshots:
  - Sprint 1 (1 week, 21 SP): login, profiles, mentor recommendations and the availability calendar.
  - Sprint 2 (2 weeks, 29 SP): conflict-free booking, scorecards with privacy control and the dashboard.
- Comments on each epic after the sprints and a reflection on the agile workflow.
