# Lab 1: Requirements and Use Case Modelling

Requirements and UML modelling for **Problem 6**, the Alumni Mentorship & Mock Interview Platform (the same problem carried through Labs 1–3).

## What I did

- **Requirements table:** 5 functional and 2 non-functional requirements, each with a type, priority, acceptance criteria and a rationale.
  - Functional: mentor recommendations by domain, skills and interests (FR-001); mentors managing their availability calendar (FR-002); booking a mentorship or mock interview session without double-booking (FR-003); post-interview scorecards (FR-004); and the student mentorship dashboard (FR-005).
  - Non-functional: privacy of profiles and feedback with reliable performance under peak load (NFR-001), and a straightforward, responsive interface (NFR-002).
- **UML use case diagram** for the platform.
- **Detailed use case UC-03, Book Mentorship / Mock Interview Session:** primary and secondary actors, preconditions, postconditions and the booking flow.
